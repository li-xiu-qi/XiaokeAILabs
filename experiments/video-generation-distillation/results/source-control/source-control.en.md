# Teacher-generated versus self-generated videos: a small controlled comparison

[简体中文](README.md) | English

Does a small model benefit specifically from examples generated by a larger teacher? A before-and-after comparison alone cannot answer that: the same training budget and prompts may also adapt the student. We trained the same Wan2.1 1.3B student for 200 steps on either teacher-generated or student-generated clips and compared both adapters with the base model.

**This sampled-frame blind review did not show the teacher-data group outperforming the base.** One AI reviewer scored five frames per clip. Mean prompt adherence / visual quality over four clips was 1.25 / 1.50 for the base, 1.00 / 1.25 for teacher data, and 1.00 / 0.75 for student data, on a 0–2 scale. These are two prompts with two seeds each, not four independent scenarios. They cannot establish general quality or motion continuity.

## Design

The six training prompts and seeds match across groups. Teacher clips were generated by Wan2.2 TI2V 5B using ComfyUI; student clips by Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B using Diffusers. Two of eight teacher clips were excluded after inspection, introducing selection bias; student clips were not further filtered. Teacher clips are 512×512 and student clips 480×480. Because the models, pipelines, and resolutions differ, this compares complete data-generation paths, not the isolated effect of model size or teacher capability.

Both students use LoRA rank 8, alpha 8, batch size 1, learning rate 0.0001, 200 steps, 49 frames, 24 fps, 480-pixel training resolution, and training seed 7357. The base and both adapters use the same student pipeline at 480×480, 49 frames, 30 sampling steps, and CFG 5. The held-out set contains two prompts and two seeds per prompt, none used in training. Matched seeds do not imply identical noise trajectories across models.

## Results and evidence

| Group | Prompt adherence / 2 | Visual quality / 2 | Motion continuity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Base | 1.25 | 1.50 | Not assessable |
| Teacher data | 1.00 | 1.25 | Not assessable |
| Student data | 1.00 | 0.75 | Not assessable |

The same reviewer, blind to A/B/C identity, scored frames 0, 12, 24, 36 and 48. For one seed of the first prompt all groups showed rings; for the other seed they were very small. For the second prompt one seed produced visible rings to varying degrees, while the other produced no clearly recognizable rings in any group. The scores describe this subjective review only; the reviewer did not watch complete videos.

See [anonymous contact sheets and original ratings](blind-review/), [held-out prompts and seeds](holdout.json), [unblinding key](blind-key.json), and [pre-unblinding hashes](blind-pre-unmask-sha256.json). From the repository root, run `python experiments/video-generation-distillation/scripts/recompute_source_control.py` to verify the rating and key hashes and recalculate the means. The hash record is described as pre-unblinding, but no independent trusted timestamp establishes when it was made. Video hashes are recorded, but cannot be checked from the repository because the MP4 files are not included. The script does not assess video quality.

## Reproducibility and licensing

The [training prompt manifest](train-prompts.json) and [teacher subset hashes](teacher-subset.json) document the data selection. Original teacher training clips, model weights and the two LoRA files are not included; the complete trainer source revision and weight snapshots were not pinned. The published contact sheets and ratings are auditable; full videos cannot be verified from this repository and the training cannot be reconstructed byte for byte. Follow the main README to rerun the method in your own environment and record software versions and weight provenance.

All twelve evaluation clips were generated from the fixed text prompts and contain no filmed people. The twelve MP4 files total 771,059 bytes and remain on the experiment device; they are not included in the repository. No model weights are included. The model cards for [Wan2.2 TI2V 5B](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B), [Wan2.1 T2V 1.3B](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B-Diffusers), and the [ComfyUI repackaging](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/Wan-2.2) identify Apache-2.0 licensing. Follow the relevant terms when redistributing models. Model cards checked 2026-11-07.
